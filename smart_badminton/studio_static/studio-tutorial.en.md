# Smart Badminton Studio manual

Studio has two editions. Browser Quick handles editing without installation. Native Pro provides the full Hybrid pipeline, trajectories, player pose and scoring. This manual primarily covers Native Pro.

In Browser Quick, choose a video, run Quick analysis, adjust clip boundaries and export MP4. Your video is not uploaded.

## 1. Understand the four types of data

1. **Source video:** the original full-quality recording. Final exports always read from this file.
2. **Proxy video:** a 720p preview generated by Studio to reduce disk reads while scrubbing.
3. **Editing timeline:** the start and end of each rally. Timeline edits change these boundaries.
4. **Visual analysis:** YOLO player pose, shuttle candidates and trajectory caches. They support review without automatically replacing a manually confirmed timeline.

## 2. Recommended workflow

> Choose folder → Open video → Calibrate court → Automatic editing → Review timeline → Save timeline → Export video

Player pose and shuttle overlays are optional. You can leave them off and continue editing manually.

Use the language selector to switch between English and Chinese. On the first visit, Studio follows your browser's preferred supported language. A manual choice is remembered. The sun/moon button switches the appearance independently of language.

## 3. Choose source videos and output location

In the project bar at the top:

1. Click **Choose** beside **Source video folder**.
2. Open the folder containing your recordings, then click **Select this folder**.
3. Choose a recording under **Match video**, then click **Open**.
4. Set the **Output folder** and an `.mp4` **Output filename**, then click **Apply**.

Studio searches recursively, excluding derived folders such as `Proxy`, `Edited` and `Analysis` so exported videos are not treated as source recordings.

## 4. Calibrate a new camera position

Expand **02 Court & trajectory**, then click **Outline court**. Select a region and click the image to add points. Drag points to refine them.

Complete these five required regions:

- **Active court:** the court used by the current match.
- **Near-side foot region:** where the near-side players' feet may appear, rather than a full-body box.
- **Far-side foot region:** where the far-side players' feet may appear.
- **Net region:** a narrow region covering the area around the net.
- **Shuttle airspace:** a generous region covering possible shuttle flight on this court.

Start with the active court, choose **Generate helper regions from the active court**, then refine the result. Use background exclusions and static false-positive regions for adjacent courts, reflections or fixed white objects.

The four orange court corners and the cyan perspective axis provide optional information. One camera cannot reliably recover the shuttle's 3D height, so Studio does not present a 2D projection as an accurate landing location.

### Rerun analysis after calibration

1. Click **Save calibration**.
2. To regenerate clips, click **Rerun rally prediction**. After confirmation, the existing timeline is backed up to `.bak` and replaced by the new predictions.
3. To update only player pose and shuttle overlays, choose **Rerun visual analysis with new calibration**. This preserves the editing timeline.

Save modified points before running analysis. Rerun controls remain disabled while calibration is unsaved.

## 5. Automatic editing

Expand **01 Automatic editing**:

- **Pre-roll:** seconds preserved before the detected rally start.
- **Post-roll:** seconds preserved after the detected rally ending.
- **Filter shuttle handoffs:** attempts to exclude gently returning the shuttle to the next server.

**Analyze current video** predicts the current timeline. If a timeline already exists, the confirmation explains replacement and backup. **Analyze new matches** processes videos without existing timelines or completion markers.

Automatic output is a draft. Review missed final hits, gentle shots, net shots, clears landing on the floor and out-of-bounds endings.

## 6. Player and timeline editing

- Click empty timeline space to position the playhead.
- Click a clip to select its rally.
- Drag a clip's left or right edge to adjust its start or end.
- Use **Start / End −1 frame / +1 frame** in the inspector for precise adjustments.
- **Split at playhead** divides the current clip.
- **Add clip** creates a clip in empty space near the playhead.
- **Delete clip** removes a clip; Undo can restore it.
- **Cut preview** plays the kept clips continuously, skipping removed gaps.

Keyboard shortcuts:

- `Space`: play / pause
- `←` / `→`: previous / next frame
- `[`: set the selected clip's start to the playhead
- `]`: set the selected clip's end to the playhead

Click **Save timeline** after editing. Save before exporting the final video.

Click **Calculate score** in the inspector. Studio first saves pending timeline changes, then calculates scores against the final clips. Recalculate after editing the timeline. Unconfirmed points stay unresolved until you review them.

## 7. Full-video visual analysis and overlays

Expand **02 Court & trajectory** and choose **Analyze full video** in the pose and trajectory card. This background job:

- Generates full-video player boxes, skeletons and wrist trails.
- Detects shuttle candidates and connects trajectories.
- Caches results in the project's `Analysis` folder.
- Preserves the editing timeline.

Hybrid combines YOLO detections and TrackNet's temporal tracking. TrackNet uses consecutive frames, while YOLO detects candidates frame by frame.

When results are ready, the player controls enable:

- **YOLO pose:** show or hide the full-video player pose overlay.
- **Debug markers:** inspect shuttle trajectory debug markers.
- **Trajectory only:** show the shuttle trail without debug markers.

These controls display existing results; clicking them does not start training. Repeating the same model does not automatically improve its accuracy.

### Background jobs and progress

After starting automatic editing, visual analysis or shuttle tracking, the header shows job progress. Open **Background jobs** to see the current video, stage, elapsed time and errors.

Jobs run in the local Studio backend independently of the current panel. Refreshing the page, closing the guide, collapsing settings or switching browser tabs will not cancel them. Reopening the page reconnects to the job status.

Keep the local service at `127.0.0.1:8765` running. If the service stops, the job is recorded as interrupted. Restart analysis after reopening Studio; cached proxies and raw detections can be reused.

## 8. Correcting trajectories is not model training

The shuttle annotation tool supports two actions:

- **Add a shuttle point:** add a manual observation at the current frame to bridge a missed detection.
- **Exclude nearby false detections:** remove nearby objects incorrectly detected as shuttlecocks.

Save annotations and rerun visual analysis to update the trajectory.

Point annotations can be exported as frame-level TrackNet coordinate labels. YOLO training still requires bounding boxes.

## 9. Motion statistics and scoring

Hit candidates, rhythm, smash candidates and entertainment scores in the inspector are exploratory and may be inaccurate. They do not modify the timeline.

A score is automatically resolved only when rally-ending trajectories or the next confirmed server provide enough evidence. Click the winning side to correct a point. Expand **Review evidence** to record the server, last hit, ending, landing side or handoff when training evidence is needed.

Recalculation uses manual outcomes to calibrate this folder's ending rules. Score corrections do not alter the timeline.

Multimodal labeling requires `SMART_BADMINTON_VLM_API_KEY` and `SMART_BADMINTON_VLM_MODEL`. Set `SMART_BADMINTON_VLM_ENDPOINT` for a compatible endpoint. Restart Studio, choose **Review unknown scores**, then accept or ignore suggestions.

The interface does not display an apparently precise but unverified landing map when a single camera cannot provide reliable 3D height.

## 10. Export a full-quality video

1. Save the timeline.
2. Confirm the output folder and filename in the header.
3. Click **Export video**.
4. Studio reads kept segments from the original recording, not an upscaled proxy.

A confirmation appears before overwriting an existing file. Studio probes NVENC, QSV, VideoToolbox and software H.264 encoders, falling back when an encoder fails.

## 11. Project files

A typical project contains:

- `Analysis/`: model features, trajectories, pose overlays and Studio working data.
- `Proxy/`: browser preview video.
- `Edited/`: final exports.
- `Calibration/`: camera-specific court configuration and backups.
- `Shuttle_Annotations/`: manual shuttle points and exclusions.

Keep the original recording. Analysis and proxy files are derived data that can be regenerated. Back up timelines and manual annotations before deleting them.

## 12. Common statuses

- **Not calibrated:** not all five required regions have been saved.
- **Not analyzed:** the model is configured, but no analysis output exists yet.
- **Update needed / Outdated:** calibration, models, raw detections or annotations are newer than the output.
- **Disabled button:** usually a missing dependency, unsaved calibration, another running job or unavailable FFmpeg. Hover over a control for its reason.
- **Many incorrect tracks:** the detector may not suit this camera angle. Exclusions fix this video; improving the detector requires suitable bounding-box training data and retraining.
- **Incorrect far-side pose:** the general pose model has limits with distance, occlusion and motion blur. Turn off overlays and continue editing.

## Final checklist

- [ ] Source and output folders are correct
- [ ] All five court regions are saved
- [ ] Automatic clips have been reviewed rally by rally
- [ ] Endings include the necessary landing, net or out-of-bounds frames
- [ ] Adjacent clips do not overlap and repeat motion
- [ ] The timeline is saved
- [ ] Output filename and folder are correct
- [ ] The exported video has been played back and checked
